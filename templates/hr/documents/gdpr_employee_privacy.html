2. Personal data we collect

  • Identity and contact details (name, address, phone, email).
  • Employment details (contract, rota, job records, performance notes).
  • Payroll and tax information (NI number, bank details, payslips).
  • Right-to-work and DBS-related information where applicable.
  • Health and safety records, training records and accident reports.
  • Photos and job completion data captured in the course of work.

3. Purposes and lawful bases

We process your data to perform your employment contract, comply with legal obligations (tax, immigration, health & safety), and for our legitimate interests in running the business safely and efficiently. Some processing requires your consent (e.g. certain optional communications).

4. Who we share data with

We may share data with HMRC, pension providers, insurers, payroll providers, DBS/identity checking services, and IT providers hosting our systems. We do not sell your data.

5. Retention

HR records are kept for as long as necessary for employment, legal and accounting purposes, typically up to six years after employment ends unless a longer period is required by law.

6. Your rights

You have rights to access, rectify, erase (in limited cases), restrict processing, object and data portability where applicable. Contact {{ employer_email }} to exercise your rights. You may complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office (ico.org.uk).

7. Security

We use appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect personal data, including access controls on staff systems and secure storage of HR documents.
