Health & Safety Policy — Staff Acknowledgment

{{ site_name }} · {{ today_formatted }}

{{ employer_name }} is committed to providing a safe working environment for employees, customers and the public. This summary forms part of our health & safety arrangements.

1. General duties

  • Take reasonable care of your own health and safety and that of others who may be affected by your work.
  • Co-operate with health & safety instructions and training.
  • Report hazards, near misses, accidents and injuries immediately to your manager.
  • Use personal protective equipment (PPE) and company equipment as instructed.

2. Working in customer homes

  • Assess the work area for slip/trip hazards, trailing cables and unsafe appliances before starting.
  • Ensure adequate ventilation when using cleaning products.
  • Follow COSHH sheets and never mix chemicals unless instructed.
  • Do not undertake electrical repairs — report faults to the customer or office.
  • Keep walkways clear and protect floors and surfaces as appropriate.

3. Manual handling and lone working

Use safe lifting techniques. Ask for assistance with heavy items. Follow lone-working procedures: share your schedule, keep your phone charged, and leave a property immediately if you feel unsafe.

4. Driving

Drive safely and legally. Do not use a handheld mobile phone while driving. Report vehicle defects promptly.

5. First aid and emergencies

Know how to contact emergency services (999) and your manager. First aid kits are provided in company vehicles / kit bags where applicable.

6. Training

You will receive role-specific training including product use, equipment operation and this policy. You must not perform tasks for which you have not been trained.

By signing below you confirm you have read this policy, understand your responsibilities, and will follow company health & safety procedures.
