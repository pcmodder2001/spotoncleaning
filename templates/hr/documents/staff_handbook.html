Employee Handbook — Acknowledgment

{{ site_name }} · {{ today_formatted }}

This handbook summarises key policies and expectations for staff at {{ site_name }}. It should be read alongside your contract of employment and any role-specific instructions from your manager.

1. Standards of conduct

  • Be polite, punctual and professional with customers and colleagues.
  • Wear company uniform and ID where provided.
  • Do not use mobile phones inappropriately while working in customer homes.
  • Never attend work under the influence of alcohol or drugs.

2. Customer properties

  • Follow access instructions and leave properties secure when leaving.
  • Report damage, defects or safety concerns immediately.
  • Do not move, borrow or remove customer belongings without permission.
  • Take before/after photos where required by job procedures.

3. Absence and lateness

Contact your manager as early as possible if you cannot attend work or will be late. Persistent lateness or unauthorised absence may result in disciplinary action.

4. Uniform and equipment

Uniform and company equipment must be kept clean and in good condition. Loss or damage through negligence may result in recovery of costs.

5. Driving and vehicles

If you drive for work you must hold a valid licence, appropriate insurance where using your own vehicle, and comply with road traffic laws. Company vehicles must be used only for authorised business purposes.

6. Social media

Do not post customer addresses, photos, keys or confidential business information on social media. Negative comments about customers or the company may be treated as misconduct.

7. Equal opportunities

{{ site_name }} is committed to equal opportunities and expects all staff to treat others with dignity and respect. Harassment, bullying or discrimination will not be tolerated.

8. Whistleblowing

If you have concerns about wrongdoing, health & safety or legal compliance, raise them with your manager or {{ employer_email }} without fear of retaliation where reports are made in good faith.

By signing below you confirm you have received, read and understood this handbook and agree to comply with its contents.
