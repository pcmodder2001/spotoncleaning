How we do it
Our service

How do we clean your appliance?

On arrival we protect your work surfaces and floors, then strip the oven of removable parts. Racks and panels are deep-cleaned while we work on the interior, door glass, and fan housing. Everything is reassembled, polished, and checked before we leave — ready to use straight away.

  3. 3
