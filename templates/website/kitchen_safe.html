Who it’s for

Peace of mind for families

A monthly kitchen reset for elderly and vulnerable people living at home in Leeds.

We deep clean the oven, hob, microwave, air fryer, dishwasher, sink, fridge, freezer and worktops, clear out expired food and cupboard debris, test smoke and CO alarms, and check the fridge temperature.

We also provide advice on any safety measures that can be taken in the kitchen. You’ll receive a kitchen safety report the same day, so you know the kitchen is clean, safe and spot-on — even when a carer or cleaner can’t be there.
