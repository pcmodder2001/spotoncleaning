{{ SITE_NAME }} (“we”, “us”, “our”) is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect personal information when you visit our website, create an account, or book our oven and appliance cleaning services in {{ SITE_LOCAL_AREA }}.

1. Who we are

{{ SITE_NAME }} is the data controller responsible for your personal data. If you have any questions about this policy or how we handle your information, contact us at {{ SITE_EMAIL }} or call {{ SITE_PHONE }}.

2. Information we collect

We may collect and process the following personal data:

  • Identity & contact details — name, email address, phone number.
  • Account information — login email and encrypted password if you register.
  • Booking details — service address, postcode, appointment date and time, services booked, notes you provide (e.g. access instructions), and pricing including discounts or promo codes.
  • Payment-related information — we do not store full card details on our servers; any payment processing is handled by our payment provider. We may retain records of amounts paid and invoices.
  • Job records — before/after photos, completion signatures, and technician notes where applicable.
  • Communications — emails, messages, and enquiry form submissions.
  • Technical data — IP address, browser type, device information, and cookies (see section 8).
  • Marketing attribution — UTM parameters or referral tags (e.g. from advertising campaigns) when you arrive on our site.

3. How we use your information

We use personal data to:

  • Process and manage bookings and appointments.
  • Confirm, remind you about, and update you on your appointments.
  • Provide customer support and respond to enquiries.
  • Manage your account and saved addresses.
  • Send service-related emails and SMS reminders (where you have provided a mobile number).
  • Improve our website, services, and scheduling.
  • Meet legal, tax, and insurance obligations.
  • Prevent fraud and protect our business.

Legal bases (UK GDPR): we rely on contract (to fulfil bookings), legitimate interests (to run and improve our business), consent (where you opt in to marketing or accept cookies where required), and legal obligation where applicable.

4. Marketing

We may send you service updates and appointment notifications by email or SMS as part of your booking. We will only send promotional marketing messages if you have opted in or where permitted by law. You can opt out of marketing at any time by contacting us or using unsubscribe links where provided.

5. Sharing your information

We do not sell your personal data. We may share information with:

  • Service providers who help us operate our website, email, SMS, hosting, or payment processing (under strict confidentiality).
  • Professional advisers (e.g. accountants, insurers) where necessary.
  • Regulators or law enforcement if required by law.

Some providers may process data outside the UK; where this occurs we ensure appropriate safeguards are in place.

6. How long we keep data

We retain personal data only as long as necessary for the purposes above. Booking and account records are typically kept for up to six years for tax, insurance, and dispute purposes, unless a longer period is required by law. Marketing preferences are kept until you withdraw consent.

7. Your rights

Under UK data protection law you have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you.
  • Request correction of inaccurate data.
  • Request erasure in certain circumstances.
  • Object to or restrict processing in certain circumstances.
  • Request data portability where applicable.
  • Withdraw consent at any time (where processing is based on consent).

To exercise your rights, contact {{ SITE_EMAIL }}. You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk.

8. Cookies & analytics

Our website uses essential cookies to keep the site working (for example login sessions, booking progress, and security). These do not require your consent.

When you choose Accept all on our cookie banner, we also use marketing cookies through Meta (Facebook) Pixel to measure advertising performance and understand how visitors find our site. If you choose Essential only, these marketing cookies are not used.

You can change your choice at any time using Cookie settings in the website footer. You can also control cookies through your browser settings.

9. Security

We take appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect your data against unauthorised access, loss, or misuse. Passwords are stored securely. No method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure, but we work to safeguard your information.

10. Children

Our services are not directed at children under 16 and we do not knowingly collect data from them. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us and we will delete it.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top will change when we do. Continued use of our website or services after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
